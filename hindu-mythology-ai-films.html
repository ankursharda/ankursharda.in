AI Cinematic Hindu Mythology Films by Anks Dharam

Anks Dharam creates original cinematic interpretations of Hindu mythology, Vedic-inspired stories, devotional chants and sacred traditions using AI animation, visual effects, editing and sound design. Created by Ankur Sharda, the films bring timeless stories to a contemporary screen.

Shani Dev: The Story of Surya Putra

The Shani Dev cinematic series explores the life of Shani Dev, traditionally associated with justice, karma and discipline. Its storytelling follows key episodes in Shani Dev's life, including his childhood and relationships within the divine family. The film Shani Dev Childhood Story — Part 1 received official selection recognition at the Neu Wave AI Film Festival.

The series combines mythological storytelling with AI-generated cinematic imagery, dramatic sound effects and music. Its visual interpretation is a creative work, not a substitute for the underlying scriptures or traditions.

Kalki Avatar: Hindu Mythology Meets Future Sci-Fi

The Kalki Avatar film project imagines a futuristic world through the lens of the final avatar of Lord Vishnu. Inspired by traditions surrounding Kalki and the cycle of the Yugas, the series blends speculative science fiction with mythological themes of time, dharma, moral decline and renewal.

Episodes explore a dying age and signs of a cosmic cycle through cinematic world-building, futuristic settings and visual effects. Fictional scenes and technologies are creative interpretations rather than literal scriptural claims.

Dashavatar: The Ten Avatars of Lord Vishnu

The Dashavatar series presents cinematic stories connected with the ten avatars of Lord Vishnu. Episodes include Vamana Avatar and the story of King Bali, and Parashurama Avatar, exploring themes of duty, humility, devotion, power and the restoration of dharma.

The channel also features devotional and narrative content connected with Narasimha Avatar and Matsya Avatar, combining accessible storytelling with stylized AI animation.

Lord Shiva, Neelkanth and Samudra Manthan

Why is Lord Shiva called Neelkanth? Anks Dharam explores the story of Samudra Manthan, the churning of the ocean, and the symbolism of Shiva holding the poison. Alongside mythology films, the channel features Shiva devotional chants and other sacred musical works.

About Anks Dharam

Anks Dharam is an independent digital cinematic platform created by Ankur Sharda. Its original productions combine AI-generated visuals, human-led story development, editing, music and sound effects. The channel is dedicated to Hindu mythology, devotional works and cinematic storytelling.
